Fracital has kept a browsing history for a few days now: the sites you open through it, listed on the History page where you can search and clear them. Until today that history only ever lived in your browser.

## What's new

You can now **back up your history to your Fracital account**. It's a switch on your account page, under _History backup_, and it is **off until you turn it on**.

With it on:

- The history on every device you sign in on is saved to your account and merged, so each device shows the same list.
- Deleting a visit or clearing history on one device removes it from the backup and from your other devices.
- Free accounts keep up to 5,000 visits; Premium keeps up to 20,000.
- Turning the switch off deletes the backup. Your local history stays.

## What we do with it

Nothing, except show it back to you. **We never sell your history, and we don't read it.** A backup is only ever read by your own signed-in devices. It isn't used for ads, statistics, moderation or anything else, and it never appears in our admin tools. Deleting your account deletes it.

## The policy

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy#history) now says all of this in its "Browsing history" item, and the "Proxy" item has been reworded: pages you open still pass through our servers to load, and we still don't log which sites you visit there.
