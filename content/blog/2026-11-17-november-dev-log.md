A round-up of what landed this week.

## Profiles

- **Banners are one shape now, 2.5:1.** When you pick an image you get a crop tool: drag the frame, zoom, and the result is rendered at 1200×480 before upload, so any photo works. GIFs keep their animation; the frame you choose is saved and applied when the banner is shown.
- **Size caps by plan.** Banners are up to 4 MB, or 15 MB with Premium, for stills and GIFs alike.
- **Listening now shows more.** The song you're playing comes with how many times you've streamed it this week, and a list of what else you played in the last seven days. Both only show when you share your listening.
- Smaller things: the profile picture no longer has a fill behind it (transparent pictures keep their shape), the social icons are smaller, and the follower counts sit on one row with them.
